Meet Maggie & The Margaret Place Massive

"Hey there, my name's Margaret Barbara Kew, but everyone around Frankton calls me Maggie."

Maggie's Place was born out of a simple, uncompromising obsession: authentic wood-fire barbecue done properly in the heart of Queenstown's southern alpine country. Located at 11 Margaret Place in the Frankton industrial area, we fired up our pits right next to our good mate Patrick at Queenstown Collision Centre — our very first customer and an absolute legend.

Today, Maggie's is the open secret of Queenstown. From local tradies grabbing their daily $15 combo to ski instructors, backcountry adventurers, and holidaymakers returning from The Remarkables, everyone gathers here for honest, hearty kai (#kapaikai).

🪵

Hardwood Smoke

Slow-burnt over seasoned Otago Cherry, Pear and Apple wood for sweet, mellow smoke.

⏰

14-Hour Patience

Pits burn all night. Low and slow until the bark is crisp and the brisket melts.

🔪

Scratch Made

Buns, chimichurri, spice rubs, sauces, and hot pies baked fresh in-house every sunrise.
